# `dailyconnect` — daily LinkedIn relationship routine

Run every weekday, before `dailyauthority`. Read first: business-profile.md, outreach-playbook.md, do-not-message.md, lead-tracker.csv.

## Config (my standing choices)
- Connection requests per day: **[3-5 in week 1, 5-7 in week 2, then 10]**. Split ~70% BUYER / ~30% REFERRER.
- Routine replies and welcomes: [DRAFT ONLY for the first 5 days, then SEND DIRECTLY]. High-value or tricky contacts: always draft and hold for my approval.
- Every first message, welcome and offer ends with my booking link (see business-profile.md).
- If LinkedIn shows "you're doing that too often" or any restriction warning: STOP sending immediately and tell me.

## HARD RULE: people I already know
Before sending ANY message, check do-not-message.md and the thread. If I have met the person, had a call, or a long conversation: DO NOT SEND. Flag them and ask me what to do.

## Voice
Write like my samples in dailyauthority-command.md. Short, warm, slightly imperfect. No long dashes, no semicolons, no "What I notice is..." openers, no three-part sentences. At most one emoji.

## Browser notes
- Always open your OWN new tab. Never take over a tab I am using.
- The messaging thread switcher often freezes: open the person's profile, click Message, check the name in the box before typing.
- If a page freezes, open a fresh tab and retry.

## The 7 steps
1. **Precheck.** Open linkedin.com/feed in a new tab, confirm I am logged in. If not, stop and tell me.
2. **Triage messages.** Read unread and recent threads. Summarise who wrote and what they want. Flag the important ones (buyers, clinic owners, time-sensitive).
3. **Reply** to everyone who needs it, following the Config above.
4. **Send connection requests** from the target list in outreach-playbook.md, top to bottom. Short personal note that names something real from their profile or posts. Never pitch in the note. Skip anyone already connected or pending.
5. **Welcome new connections** within 24h of accept. Thank them, ask one genuine question about their practice, end with the booking link. Welcomes owed from yesterday are Priority 1.
6. **Follow up** once with people who went quiet 7-10 days ago. Max two nudges per person ever.
7. **Update lead-tracker.csv** with every request, accept and reply (tier and pipeline tagged).

## End-of-run report
Messages summarised + who's important · replies sent · requests sent (with tier) · welcomes sent · follow-ups · anything held for my approval · buyer names left on the list.
