# `dailyauthority` — daily LinkedIn visibility routine

Run every weekday, right after `dailyconnect`.

## Config
- Post days: **Tue, Wed, Thu** (or when I say "post today"). One post per day max.
- Every other week: one longer LinkedIn article (800-1,500 words), always held for my approval.
- Comments per day: **[3-5 in week 1, 5-7 in week 2, then 10]**, total for the day.
- Post from my personal profile. Language: match the audience (local clinics in [Bulgarian], international in English).
- Text-first posts. If a post needs an image, prepare it and leave the last click to me.

## Safety valve: HOLD for my approval, never auto-publish
- anything with a medical, health-outcome or results claim
- any number about revenue, price or return
- any patient or clinic story (needs written consent)
- anything political, tragic or sensitive

## A. POST block (post days only)
1. Read content-log.md. Don't repeat the last pillar.
2. Draft 1 post, 600-1,300 characters, one idea, strong first line, end with a question doctors want to answer.
   - **P1 Practice growth** (weight heavily): a new self-pay service line, rooms and staff you already have, returning patients.
   - **P2 After the birth** (weight heavily): what happens to pelvic floor care after the 6-week check, and who owns it.
   - **P3 Do we really care?**: patients rarely mention leaks or intimacy problems. Does anyone ask?
   - **P4 The evidence, plainly**: what published studies show, source linked. Never invent a statistic.
   - **P5 Inside a clinic**: how a practice set up the service. Consent only.
   - **P6 Myths**: "leaking is normal after kids", "it's just ageing", "surgery is the only fix".
3. Run the safety valve. Clean: publish (or draft only in week 1). Otherwise hold and show me.
4. Add to content-log.md: date, pillar, language, first line.

## B. ENGAGEMENT block (every run)
Mix: ~6 BUYER (gynecology clinic owners, urogynecologists, women's health clinics), ~3 REFERRER (pelvic floor physios, midwives), ~1 existing connection.
- Comment on buyers BEFORE sending them a connection request, so my name is familiar.
- Read comment-log.md first. Never comment on the same person twice within 15 days. Append every comment after posting.
- Spread comments out with natural gaps. Never back to back.
- If the feed doesn't have enough good posts, post fewer and say so. Never pad.

## Quality bar
- Each comment adds something the post didn't say: a reframe, a lived observation, a real question.
- 1-3 sentences. Generous, never correcting or one-upping the author.
- NEVER: "Great post", "So true", "Thanks for sharing", any pitch, link, price, "DM me", medical claims, hashtags, emoji strings, comments on sensitive or grief posts.
- Vary the angle across the batch. Never open two comments the same way.

## Voice: write like me, not like an AI
My real messages (paste 5-10, keep the typos):

> [sample 2]
> [sample 3]

- No long dashes. No semicolons. Short. Imperfect is fine. At most one emoji.
- Self-check: if it sounds like a polished LinkedIn thought-leader, rewrite it shorter and rougher.

## End-of-run report
Post published or held (full text) · comments posted with name and full text, count out of the daily target · anything held for approval.
