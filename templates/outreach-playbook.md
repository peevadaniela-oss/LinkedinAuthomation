# Outreach playbook

## Pipelines
- **BUYER** (~70% of daily effort): people who could put a chair in their practice.
- **REFERRER** (~30%): people who send patients to a practice that has one.

## Tiers
| Tier | Who | Pipeline | What I offer them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Owners / medical directors of private gynecology, OB-GYN clinics, maternity hospitals | Buyer | Free demo |
| 1 | Urogynecologists and urologists in private practice | Buyer | Free demo |
| 2 | Aesthetic and intimate-health clinics, women's health centres, medical spas | Buyer | Call, then demo |
| 2 | Practice managers, clinic group operations leads | Buyer | Call |
| 3 | Pelvic floor physiotherapists, midwives, doulas, postpartum coaches | Referrer | Relationship, list of clinics near them |
| 3 | Investors / franchise groups in women's health | Buyer | Call |

If a person's tier is unclear, do NOT guess. Hold them and ask me.

When fewer than 20 names are left "not contacted", tell me and suggest the next region.

## Warm signals (check before sending a request)
Posts or comments about pelvic floor, incontinence, postpartum recovery, women's health, practice growth. Active weekly beats big but silent.

## Message templates (Claude adapts them, never copies word for word)

**Connection note (max 200 characters, no pitch)**
> Hi [name], saw your post about [real detail]. I work with gynecology practices on pelvic floor care, would be glad to connect.

**Welcome after accept (within 24h)**
> Thanks for connecting [name] :) Curious, how do you handle pelvic floor follow up after the 6 week check at [clinic]? [booking link]

**Follow-up (once, 7-10 days later)**
> Hi [name], just coming back to this. If it's useful I can show you how a few clinics set up a pelvic floor service, 20 min. [booking link]

Max two nudges per person, ever.
